# Privacy Policy for MyVoiceLab

**Last Updated: December 27, 2024**

## Introduction

MyVoiceLab ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our MyVoiceLab mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Audio and Video Data

- **What we collect**: Audio and video files you import into the App for voice transformation.
- **How we use it**: Your audio/video is processed on our secure servers to apply voice transformations. The audio is sent to our backend servers for AI processing and returned to your device.
- **Storage**: We do not permanently store your audio or video files on our servers. Files are processed and immediately deleted after the transformation is complete.

### 2. Device Information

- **What we collect**: Anonymous device identifiers, device type, operating system version, and app version.
- **How we use it**: To improve app performance, fix bugs, and understand how users interact with our App.

### 3. Usage Analytics

We use third-party analytics services to understand how users interact with our App:

**Firebase Analytics (Google)**
- App usage patterns and user flows
- Feature usage statistics
- Session duration and frequency
- Crash reports and error logs

**Amplitude**
- User engagement metrics
- Feature adoption tracking
- Conversion analytics

These services collect anonymous usage data and do not identify you personally.

### 4. Purchase Information

- **What we collect**: Subscription status, purchase dates, and transaction identifiers (provided by Apple).
- **How we use it**: To manage your subscription, provide access to premium features, and handle billing inquiries.
- **Note**: We do not have access to your payment details (credit card numbers, etc.). All payments are processed securely by Apple.

## Subscriptions

### Subscription Terms

- Payment will be charged to your Apple ID account at confirmation of purchase
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period
- Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period
- You can manage and cancel your subscriptions by going to your App Store account settings after purchase

### How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your subscription at any time. To cancel:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone or iPad
2. Tap your **Apple ID** (your name at the top)
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **MyVoiceLab**
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

Alternatively:
1. Open the **App Store** app
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **MyVoiceLab** and tap **Cancel Subscription**

**Important**:
- Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period
- You will retain access to premium features until the end of your paid period
- Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription

### Refunds

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund:

1. Go to [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
2. Sign in with your Apple ID
3. Select "Request a refund"
4. Choose the reason for your refund
5. Select the MyVoiceLab subscription or purchase
6. Submit your request

**Note**: Refund decisions are made by Apple at their discretion. We cannot process refunds directly. For refund status, contact Apple Support.

### 5. Crash and Performance Data

- **What we collect**: Crash logs, performance metrics, and error reports via Firebase Crashlytics.
- **How we use it**: To identify and fix bugs, improve app stability, and enhance user experience.

## How We Use Your Information
